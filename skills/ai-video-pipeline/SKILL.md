# AI Video Pipeline

Short video rewards consistency and a real point of view. AI makes the consistency cheap; it can't supply the point of view. Done means a batch of scripts the founder can read naturally, a footage plan per video, captions, and the correct AI labels, ready to schedule.

Human + AI Module 3. Free course: humanai.news/learn/ai-video-pipeline. Setup facts, prices and sources: `references/setup.md` (dated; load it before quoting any price or plan name).

## Decide: film, AI, or a mix

| Situation | Default |
|---|---|
| The founder's face and personality ARE the brand | Film on a phone. Use AI only for cutaway visuals. |
| Founder is camera-shy but has the expertise | Voiceover plus AI visuals, or an avatar of the founder's own face with consent |
| Product or process business | Screen recordings or product shots, AI for openers and transitions |

Hard rule: an avatar or voice clone only of a person who has given written consent, ideally the founder themselves. Never a real person's likeness without it.

## Script a batch

1. Take one idea or the week's notes. Find five angles: a mistake, a myth, a how-to, a story, a number.
2. For each, three hook options under ~10 words that state a specific outcome or tension in the first two seconds. No "In today's video".
3. Script 30 to 60 seconds: hook, one point, one example, one line to close or a reason to follow. Written to be said, not read: short sentences, the founder's words from `voice-profile.md`.
4. Mark every stat or claim you can't source as `[CHECK]`.
5. Footage plan per video: what's on screen in each beat (founder to camera, AI cutaway, screen, b-roll).
6. The founder reads each script aloud once. Anything they stumble on gets rewritten in their words.

## Captions and labels

Captions on every video (most short video is watched muted). Then the platform labels, which change often, so check `references/setup.md` for current wording:
- **YouTube:** disclose realistic altered or synthetic content (a real person appearing to say something they didn't, realistic fake scenes). AI-written scripts and captions don't need it. When in doubt with a realistic avatar, disclose.
- **TikTok and Meta:** label realistic AI-generated video; platforms may auto-label and penalise missing labels.
Keep a simple consent record for any likeness used.

## Weekly routine and measuring

One 2 to 3 hour batch session a week, then schedule the week in YouTube Studio. Monthly, look at retention (where people swipe away) more than views. The first three seconds decide most of it, so rewrite hooks first. Log winning hook patterns in `learnings.md`.
