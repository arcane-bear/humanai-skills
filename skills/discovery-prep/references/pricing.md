# Human + AI pricing bands

Load before writing any price in a plan. These are the published bands on humanai.news/ai-implementation as of 2026-11-25. If the site has changed since, the site wins.

| Build | Fixed price band | What moves the price |
|---|---|---|
| Module 1: Voice note to newsletter | $2,000 – $3,500 | Higher end if we migrate an existing list |
| Module 2: Website that updates itself | $2,000 – $4,000 | The CMS, and how many page types we publish to |
| Module 3: AI video pipeline | $3,000 – $5,500 | Formats, avatar setup, channels we stage for |
| Module 4: Custom ops agents | $3,500 – $8,500+ | Hours, and the number of systems connected |
| Full content engine (Modules 1–3) | $6,500 – $10,500 | Delivered together with one brand-voice setup |
| Monthly care plan | $1,250 – $2,500 / month | Monitoring, model updates, 2 coaching sessions, 1 small improvement a month |

Terms: 50% to start, 50% at launch and handover. Care plan starts the month after launch, 30 days' notice to cancel. Software is billed to the client's own accounts.
